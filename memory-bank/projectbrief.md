# Vantia Project Brief

Vantia is an AI-powered Universal Opportunity Engine that replaces
predatory job boards and recruitment agencies with a direct, verified
pipeline for international job applications, scholarship matching,
credential equivalence, ATS scoring, CV improvement, cover letter
generation, and anti-fraud guardrails.

Core promise: "Every application Vantia produces is targeted, verified,
and free of third-party intermediaries."

Target users: skilled tradespeople and graduates from Nigeria, Kenya,
Ghana, India, Philippines, Brazil, Pakistan, Bangladesh seeking
relocation to UK, DE, IE, SE, CA, AU.

Business model: freemium with token-based credit system.

Deployment: Render free tier (backend), Vercel Hobby (frontend),
Supabase free tier (database + auth + storage). No paid infrastructure.
