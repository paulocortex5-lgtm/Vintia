# Product Context

## Problem
Skilled workers and graduates from developing countries are exploited by
predatory recruitment agencies charging illegal fees, fake job postings,
and non-sponsoring employers wasting applicant time.

## Solution
Vantia verifies employers against government visa-sponsor registers,
blocks fee-charging postings, provides ATS scoring so applicants can see
their match quality, and generates tailored CV improvements and cover
letters aligned to specific job descriptions.

## User Journey
1. Sign up with email or OAuth
2. Land on dashboard with 10,000 free credits
3. Upload CV to private workspace
4. Paste job URL or description
5. Get ATS score (0-100) with 12-category breakdown
6. Improve CV with AI (8,000 credits)
7. Generate cover letter (5,000 credits)
8. Download both as PDF
9. Buy more credits with Paddle when needed
